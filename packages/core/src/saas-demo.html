About this demo

A typical SaaS layout has a top navbar and a left sidebar, with the customer's primary content flowing inside a main region that often extends below the fold. We want the toast to behave well inside that layout — not slide under the navbar, not overlap the sidebar, and never escape the viewport even when the page is scrolled.
